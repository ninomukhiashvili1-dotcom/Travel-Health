## Brand & Style

This design system translates the intimacy of expedition travel into an elevated digital health and wellness compass. It targets discerning global travelers, digital nomads, and wellness enthusiasts who prioritize physiological vitality, circadian alignment, and cognitive resilience while crossing borders.

The aesthetic fuses **Luxury Travel Editorial** with **Minimalist Tech Interface**:
- **Editorial Sophistication:** Expansive white space, deliberate proportioning, sculpted typographic hierarchy, and museum-grade cataloguing for destinations and health protocols.
- **Modern Wellness Calm:** Soothing chromatic washes of porcelain and sage balanced by deeply grounded twilight indigos.
- **Tactile Precision & Glassmorphism:** Translucent floating navigation pills, polished metric displays, frosted glass pill containers, and softly radiused surfaces that feel organic yet engineered.

## Colors

The palette directly honors the brand identity while structuring a serene yet punchy visual hierarchy:

- **Twilight Blue (`#3C2F80`):** The primary anchor. Represents the twilight horizon, maritime depth, and quiet authority. Used for primary interactive actions, high-emphasis branding elements, and primary headers.
- **Hyacinth Blue (`#7D52F4`):** The kinetic secondary electric violet. Provides vitality, active status highlights, biometric alerts, and focus rings.
- **Sage Travel Accent (`#52796F`):** The restorative tertiary tone. Applied to health metrics, circadian recovery states, botanical formulations, and adaptive status tags. Accompanied by light sage tint washes (`#EBF3EE`).
- **Deep Slate Neutral (`#121026`):** The foundational dark neutral for maximum contrast typography, high-density charts, and icon line work.
- **Chalk White Canvas (`#F7F7FA`):** A cool, porcelain background tone with an ultra-subtle lavender undertone that softens digital glare and replaces harsh pure white canvas fills.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for elevated surface cards, modal trays, and frosted layers.
- **Warm Sand (`#F4EFEA`):** Ambient accent for travel kit modules and grounding warmth.

## Typography

The type system utilizes **Roboto Flex** across all tiers, drawing on its optical versatility and mechanical purity. The aesthetic avoids cold austerity by pairing lighter weights (`300` Light) at scale with tightly tracked, confident metadata labels:

- **Editorial Headlines (`display-lg`, `headline-lg`):** Rendered in slender `300` and `400` weights with negative letter-spacing for a luxury publication editorial cadence.
- **Metadata Labels (`label-caps`):** Set in all-caps, boldened (`600`), and widely tracked (`+0.12em`). Applied above section titles, coordinates, flight indicators, and health tags.
- **Biometric Numerics (`label-metric`):** Sculpted for rapid comprehension across biometric readouts (sleep tracking, hydration, jet lag circadian phases) with airy, uncrowded line-height.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored in an editorial asymmetric grid designed to evoke the composition of luxury travel journals:

- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column fluid grid, `3rem` (48px) page margins, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Asymmetric span pairs (such as 7 cols / 5 cols or 8 cols / 4 cols) balance immersive travel imagery against health and protocol metric cards.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` (32px) margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Content blocks collapse into balanced side-by-side modules.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` (20px) margins and `1rem` (16px) gutters. Modular stacks maintain rapid one-hand reachability, with horizontal-scroll carousels for multi-metric trackers.
- **Rhythm Philosophy:** Generous internal padding within cards (`space-xl` for desktop dashboards, `space-lg` for mobile cards) preserves atmospheric whitespace and avoids clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined blend of **tonal surface stacking**, **frosted glassmorphism**, and **ambient twilight shadows**:

- **Canvas Tier:** `#F7F7FA` (Chalk White) provides the ambient baseline.
- **Surface Elevation (Cards & Panels):** Solid `#FFFFFF` surfaces paired with a diffused ambient shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(60, 47, 128, 0.04), 0 1px 3px rgba(18, 16, 38, 0.02)`. The slight indigo undertone creates harmony with Twilight Blue.
- **Glassmorphic Overlays (Navigation Bars, Status Floating Pills):** Semi-transparent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.78)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a whisper border `rgba(60, 47, 128, 0.08)`.
- **Active / Popover Layer (Drawers, Diagnostics):** `0 20px 48px rgba(60, 47, 128, 0.12)`, grounding user focus directly on health metrics and actions.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a supple, high-radius curvature language inspired by ergonomic travel accessories and smooth sea-worn stones:

- **Base Radius (`rounded-md`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Form controls, inputs, and small badges.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Metric widgets, inner card containers, and selection chips.
- **Feature Card Radius (`rounded-xl` / `rounded-2xl`, 1.5rem to 2rem):** Editorial feature cards, wellness overview panels, and modal bottom sheets.
- **Pill Geometry (`rounded-full`):** Floating navigation pills, filter badges, and primary action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Twilight Blue (`#3C2F80`) fill, Pure White text, `rounded-full` contour, with `padding: 0.75rem 1.75rem`. Hover triggers a smooth lift with an ambient shadow shift and a subtle 4% luminance rise.
- **Secondary / Accent:** Hyacinth Blue (`#7D52F4`) ghost button or solid pill for real-time status actions and syncing prompts.
- **Tertiary / Botanical:** Soft sage background (`#EBF3EE`) with deep sage text (`#52796F`), ideal for natural remedies, sleep routines, and low-urgency triggers.

### Metric Chips & Status Badges
- **Circadian / Health Indicators:** Pill-shaped tags (`label-caps`) with 6px circular status dots. Healthy status takes Sage (`#52796F`), jet lag alerts feature Hyacinth Blue (`#7D52F4`), and flight coordinates feature muted deep slate.
- **Interactive Filter Chips:** `#FFFFFF` background with 1px border `rgba(60, 47, 128, 0.12)`. When selected, transitions to Twilight Blue fill with Pure White text.

### Input Fields
- Understated input frames with `#FFFFFF` background, `0.75rem` rounded corners, and a border of `rgba(60, 47, 128, 0.15)`.
- Active focus state deploys a `2px` ring in Hyacinth Blue (`#7D52F4`) with zero layout shift.
- Floating `label-caps` in deep slate neutral guide input effortlessly.

### Cards
- **Editorial Cards:** Asymmetric ratios, soft `1.5rem` corners, Pure White canvas, housing full-bleed atmospheric photography on one edge and generous text with `display-lg` numbers on the opposite side.
- **Health Metric Cards:** White cards with frosted Sage or Hyacinth accent headers, micro-sparkline graphs, and bold, readable numeric values (`label-metric`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Smooth rounded square checkboxes (4px radius) and circular radio controls. Active state fills with Twilight Blue with crisp white interior marks.

### Navigation Pill
- A floating bottom-centered or top-aligned dock. Frosted glass finish (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` with 20px blur), minimal icon outlines, and an active pill indicator glowing in Hyacinth Blue.
